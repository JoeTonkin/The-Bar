How progression works

Lock in the reps you actually get on every set. Hit the target range on all sets → weight goes up next time.
Fall short on any set → repeat the same weight.
Miss two sessions running on a lift → drops 5% and builds back up.
Deload weeks (5 & 10, or any week you flag as "feeling wiped") auto-load at 60%, 2 sets, no progression — just move and recover.

Adjusting on the fly

"Short on time" trims sets and drops any exercise whose muscle group is already covered elsewhere this week.
Any exercise can be skipped for just today — it'll come back on its normal day next time.
On a 4-session week, the "add exercise" list flags a lift with ⚠️ if it'd be the 3rd time that muscle group gets trained that week.
On a 2-session week, you'll see suggested add-ons for muscle groups Push/Pull don't otherwise cover (glutes, calves).
